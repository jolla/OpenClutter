How it works
  1. Draw an area on the map and click Export. You get a zip file.
  2. In your Hamina project, open the breadcrumb dropdown, choose Import / Export (third icon), and import the zip as OpenIntent.
  3. When the import finishes, click Copy terrain here and paste it into Hamina.
