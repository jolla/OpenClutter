## 1.2

- **1.2.50** Bug fix on every host: light poles sit on sloped terrain. A pole is one point, and the terrain seat needed at least two, so on a hill (20 m or more of relief) every pole stayed on the floor and was buried in the Copy terrain floors. Each pole now takes the ground under its own 0.3 m width as bottom_height, the same way buildings, RVs and fences are seated. Flat sites are unchanged.
- **1.2.49** Bug fix on every host: Cancel frees the export machine. When the page cancels an export, the worker process now exits about half a second after it learns of the cancel, even if the connection that started the job was already closed, and it turns away new exports until it has stopped, so a cancelled job can no longer run on to Packing zip beside the next export. Exports that are not cancelled are unchanged.
- **1.2.47** Bug fix on every host: Copy terrain raised and sloped floors use 20 dB/m at 5 GHz as a solid volume (slabOnly false), so a path through ground is attenuated. They were 0 dB/m before.
- **1.2.43** Overture buildings read release 2026-10-21.0 (the row-group index is rebuilt from that release). Overture deletes old releases, and when 2026-09-23.1 goes away every Overture read would be a 404 and Overture-only roofs such as the Las Vegas Sphere would leave every export. This is a fix and runs on every host. Building outlines follow the new release, so a few footprints can differ where Overture updated its data.
- **1.2.42** Tree crowns on /dev hosts: the out-of-date lidar test needs bright roof evidence. A lidar crown with no leaves on the current photo counts toward the test only when at least 15% of it reads as a bright roof, a vehicle top or pale ground, and a park counts as out of date only when at least 40% of its leafless crowns show that evidence. Dense leaf-on canopy that the leaf test misses (dull or hazy green) no longer turns on photo reshaping, so those crowns keep their lidar outlines: at Traverse City KOA, 1.2.39 cut tree cover from 4,718 to 2,370 m2 and now keeps 4,655 m2. Pismo Coast Village still reads as out of date and is unchanged. Gated to /dev hosts by export-mode.js; production exports are unchanged.
- **1.2.41** Production batch 1: the /dev export rules run on every host. export-mode.js now turns on siteTypes (the 1.2.18 RV park, campground and marina rules and every later RV detection fix), bigBudget (5.8 MB of OpenIntent JSON, JSON plus map image at most 9.7 MB), photoCanopy for every host, and RV hollow shells and park context for every request that does not send rvShell:false or parkContext:false. Cancel on the Exporting button stops the job on the server on every page. Each switch stays in the code, so one rule can go back to /dev only with a one-line change. The 1.2.39 photo-shaped tree crowns (photoCrowns) stay on /dev hosts: at Traverse City KOA their stale-lidar test read 54% of crowns as leafless in a leafy campground and cut real dense canopy. The /dev test overrides (area cap, JSON budget, terrain floors, legacy fetch), the imagery picker and the /dev stats stay on /dev hosts.
- **1.2.40** Promotion merge of /dev 1.2.39 into production (batch 1). No rule change: every /dev export rule is still gated to /dev hosts by export-mode.js, so production exports match 1.2.36. Production now serves the /dev page and runs the current code on the Netlify fallback path.
- **1.2.39** Tree crowns and RV detection on /dev hosts. Tree crowns: inside mapped RV parks, campgrounds and mobile home parks, a park whose lidar is out of date (at least 35% of 20 or more lidar crowns there show no leaves on the current photo) gets its crowns trimmed to the leaves on the photo and split into single trees, and crowns with no leaves are left out. In other parks crowns keep their lidar outline, and a crown with no leaves is left out only when at least half of it reads as a roof, a vehicle top or bare ground. At Pismo Coast Village tree cover drops from 7.4% to 3.6% (traced crowns cover about 4.6%). RVs: one roadside RV box per unit (overlapping boxes from the same unit are merged), no worst-case pad fills within 16 m of a road junction, a pad density gate of 1.8 RVs per 100 m of park road, tan ground and dull pale patches on a dim photo no longer read as roadside RV roofs, and a rooftop vent cue joins small white roof pieces with 3 or more vent or AC gaps into one RV-sized box. All of this is gated to /dev hosts by export-mode.js; production exports are unchanged.
- **1.2.38** Cancel stops the export on the server too (/dev page). Before, Cancel on the Exporting button only stopped the page from waiting while the worker ran the job to its end. The /dev page now also calls a new cancel endpoint (POST /api/export-cancel with the job id, the same key the status check uses). It marks the job cancelled, a final state that stores no file, and asks the Fly machine running it to stop. The worker also checks for the mark on every 10 s heartbeat, answers right away, and its process exits so map, photo and lidar fetches and CPU threads stop and the machine is free for the next export. A job cancelled before it reaches the worker never starts, and the Netlify fallback never runs a cancelled job again or stores its file. Production pages do not call the endpoint; exports that are not cancelled are unchanged on every host.
- **1.2.37** Tree cover in RV parks on /dev hosts. The 2018 USGS lidar measured the RVs, trailers and awnings parked on the day of the flight as tree crowns, so Pismo Coast Village showed solid green blocks over the RV rows (27% foliage cover where traced crowns cover about 4.8%). Inside a mapped RV park, campground or mobile home park, on a leaf-on photo, a lidar canopy cell with no foliage texture is now cleared when it is lower than a road vehicle (5 m) or the photo shows bare ground or a roof, and a traced crown with foliage on under 20% of its outline is dropped. Bare branches and deep shade with no bright roof keep a cell or crown, so leaf-off hardwoods and shaded crowns stay. Nothing is added. At Pismo, foliage cover drops from 27% to 7.7%, the share of foliage on real crowns rises from 17% to 57% and real crowns found stay at 85%. The site-type bench scores Pismo trees against crowns hand drawn on the current photo (labels stay outside the repo); scored that way over the 12 sites, 1.2.36 is 64.30 and 1.2.37 is 64.60. Production exports keep the 1.2.17 canopy.
