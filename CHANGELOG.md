## 1.2

- **1.2.17** Production exports use the 1.2.15 and 1.2.16 fetch paths too: Overpass retries and fallback across public servers, and the map waits for a sharper plate and tiles large plates. On /dev, `legacyFetch: true` still replays the earlier paths.
- **1.2.16** Fixes a /dev worker crash on the second export per machine: the area cache took its own blob reads (whose keys now name the street-map source) for street-map reads and called itself until the stack overflowed. The cache now matches source hosts only and never wraps Netlify blob traffic.
- **1.2.15** Street-map reads on /dev go through one Overpass client: overpass-api.de, then private.coffee, then VK Maps, with one extra request beside an answer slower than 15 s, a pause after 429 that honours Retry-After and the /api/status slot time (at least the 30 s the main server asks for), backoff with jitter after 5xx, timeouts and runtime-error answers, at most 2 or 3 requests in flight per server, identical reads merged, and a 10 minute copy beside the 6 h OSM cache. When every server still misses, the outdoor clutter warning stays. The /dev map no longer keeps the coarsest plate when the sharpest fails fast: a coarser plate waits up to 20 s for a sharper one still on the wire, and plates from 2000 px are fetched as tiles (the Las Vegas Sphere draw goes from 663 px to 2211 px). Production keeps the earlier paths until promotion.
- **1.2.14** Building outlines with no roof shadow on the photo are left out (stale footprints of demolished buildings), when the sun direction and shadow scale are read confidently from surveyed roofs and the outline shows no edge, roof or raised surface. Stacked outdoor storage is read from wide shadow runs on paved yards beside large roofs and drawn as Storage blocks 2.5 to 9 m tall; empty pavement, rail ballast and car lots stay empty. Lidar storage yards are now drawn (they were dropped before). Lidar crowns over ground the leaf-on photo shows as open are dropped when the flight is 4 or more years old. Bench adds home-depot-fdc-tx, a marina and a state fairground outside the gate mean.
- **1.2.13** Changing any setting after an export returns the button from Copy terrain to Export, so the next click re-runs the export with the new settings. Opening or closing Settings does not.
- **1.2.12** NAIP and national aerial plates are color-leveled toward the Esri range before the photo checks, so haze no longer clears real canopy (Auto bench 69.4 to 75.2, Esri unchanged). Detection only; the image in the zip is untouched.
- **1.2.11** Every data source is credited in the zip (`ATTRIBUTION.txt`) and on the page (`/licenses.html`), and sources whose terms do not fit are dropped. See [DATA-LICENSES.md](DATA-LICENSES.md).
- **1.2.10** Overture buildings read release 2026-09-23.1. Overture deleted 2026-08-19.0, every read was a 404, and Overture-only roofs (the Las Vegas Sphere) were missing from every export.
- **1.2.9** The aerial Source choice is read only on /dev hosts; production always uses Esri. The canopy bench takes `CANOPY_BENCH_SOURCE`.
- **1.2.8** /dev aerial Source picker: Esri (default), Auto, NAIP (US), or National (swisstopo, PDOK, NLS, IGN). A source that misses the area falls back to Esri, and the source, capture date and credit are in Details and the zip.
- **1.2.7** A flat block of lidar cells a few meters across, alone in the open, is a field light's lamp bank, drawn as a pole at the lidar height. Read only where two lidar cells fit inside 20 m2 and 5 m, and under 40 m, so coarse surveys keep their crowns.
- **1.2.6** A lone stemless crown piece that the aerial shows as open ground is dropped (lidar specks over streets and lots).
- **1.2.5** A crown piece cut at a roof edge under the 3 m floor is dropped instead of grown to a 3 m square.
- **1.2.4** Poles keep a measured or tagged height from 3 to 45 m. A mapped pole on a lidar spike takes the lidar height.
- **1.2.3** Lidar canopy on every host. A survey that clips the draw edge does not win over a full one, and stands up to 10 ha split per tree instead of 42 m blocks.
- **1.2.2** The area readout adds acres (one decimal under 10).
- **1.2.1** /dev moves to 1.2.1 to match the next production release. `npm run bump` moves the 1.2.x patch number.
- **1.2.0** Production release of /dev v1.1.219: improved tree modeling, light poles, towers and domes at real height, Download again for 24 hours, place suggestions, and on-page instructions.
